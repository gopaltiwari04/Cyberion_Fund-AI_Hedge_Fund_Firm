<P STYLE="margin-top:12pt; margin-bottom:0pt; text-indent:4%; font-size:10pt; font-family:Times New Roman"><B>REDMOND, Wash. &#8212; May 14, 2026</B> &#8212; Microsoft Corp. on Thursday announced the appointment of Carmine Di Sibio, former global

our customers while advancing Microsoft&#8217;s mission to empower every person and organization to achieve more. With Di Sibio&#8217;s appointment, the Microsoft board of directors has been expanded to 13&nbsp;members. </P>
